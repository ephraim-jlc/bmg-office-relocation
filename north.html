Page-wide rules
  • This is the North variant (Leeds, Manchester and the wider North of England). Same layout as the other variant; the location copy, phone, social proof, recent moves, logo strip and local office table differ.
  • Page title: Office relocation in the North | Free site survey | Business Moves Group
    Meta description: Planning an office move in Leeds, Manchester or across the North? Book a free site survey with a written report and recommendations, with no obligation to move with us.
  • <meta name="robots" content="noindex">, excluded from XML sitemap, no links to it from the main site, canonical to itself.
  • No main site navigation. Only logo, phone and CTA in the header, so there are no exits other than the Google Business Profile link.
  • Every button on the page scrolls to the form (desktop) or jumps to it (mobile). The only alternative action is calling.
  • Frames are drawn at 1280px wide. Content column is narrow (about 760px) like the BMG homepage; wider sections use about 960px. The hero frame keeps its 16:9 ratio at any width. Below about 1600px viewport width the frames shrink to fit beside the notes rather than staying at 1280.
Dev notes
iStyle references from businessmoves.com: Poppins, pill buttons, round icon buttons, grey section bands, 3-column tile grids.
Desktop: page frames are 1280px wide. The first frame (header + hero) is exactly 16:9 (1280 x 720), i.e. the above-the-fold view.

1Header. Sticky on scroll. Phone is a tel: link and fires a click-to-call event. Button anchors to the form. Phone number shown follows the location variant (see Local offices).
2Hero. Headline, subhead and form side by side. Header + hero together fill the 1280x720 (16:9) fold, so the whole form and its button must be visible without scrolling. On mobile the form drops below the subhead.
3Review stars. New element, not in the copy doc. Competitors show ratings in the hero. Rating, count and Google Business Profile link to come from BMG.
4Form. 5 fields, all required except the dropdown. Submit fires the lead-form event, then redirects to the thank-you page. Spam protection must not use a visible CAPTCHA.
5Trust badges. To check. The ISO 9001, 14001 and 45001 certifications (and BAR membership) must be verified with BMG and displayed properly using the official certification marks, not text or placeholders. Confirm the certificates are current and which accreditations to show (the supplied logo set further down is a different group). The text line underneath is the fallback and alt text.
